## Construction corrections (evidence-driven)

Two defects were found by measurement and fixed; both are reported rather than hidden:

1. **`railway=platform` was entering the graph** (1,074 ways). A blocklist filter let non-track ways in, splitting the network into ~1,150 components; 312 of 394 segments fell back to straight lines. An allowlist (`railway=rail`) reduced this to 21 components.
2. **`service=crossover|siding|spur` was excluded**, although on double track these are the only links between the parallel tracks. Excluding `yard` alone cut suspect segments from 88 to 8 and raised fully-routed trips from 94 to 238.
